📘 Marks Workbook Format & Grading Contract Guidelines

The uploaded Excel file should contain three columns: BITS ID, Course, and Total Marks. Total marks should be on a 0–100 scale. Fractional marks will automatically be rounded to the nearest integer (e.g., 80.2 → 81, 79.4 → 79) in accordance with BITS academic grading policy.

Note on Absentee (NC) Students: Students who did not appear for the trimester-end examination and are to be awarded an NC grade should NOT be included in the file.
